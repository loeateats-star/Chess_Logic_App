Three ways to help

🧑‍🏫

Volunteer as a mentor

No coaching license required — just a willingness to sit across the board from a kid for an hour a week. We provide the curriculum.

🏫

Start a chapter

Bring the full program — puzzles, video curriculum, and progress tracking — to your school or after-school site.

📣

Spread the word

Know a Title I school, a librarian, or an after-school program that would want this? An introduction is worth more than you'd think.

Starting a Chapter

Four steps to launch

1

Reach out

Tell us about your school or community — size, age group, and what resources you already have.

2

Get set up

We walk you through the curriculum, the puzzle trainer, and how to onboard your first group of students — free, no equipment required beyond a screen.

3

Launch your first cohort

Start small — even a handful of students meeting once a week is a chapter.

4

Grow from there

Track progress on the dashboard, add mentors as you go, and let us know what you need next.
