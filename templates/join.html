Bring chess to your community.

Synapchess grows through people, not ad spend: coaches, teachers and volunteers who believe every kid deserves to learn to think five moves ahead.

Get in touch →

Three ways to help.

  • Volunteer as a mentor

    No coaching license needed, just a willingness to sit across the board from a kid for an hour a week. We provide the curriculum.

  • Start a chapter

    Bring the full program, puzzles, video lessons and progress tracking, to your school or after-school site.

  • Spread the word

    Know a Title I school, a librarian or an after-school program that would want this? An introduction is worth more than you would think.

Starting a chapter.

  1. Reach out

    Tell us about your school or community: size, age group and what you already have.

  2. Get set up

    We walk you through the lessons, the puzzle trainer and onboarding your first students. Free, and a screen is all you need.

  3. Launch your first group

    Start small. Even a handful of students meeting once a week is a chapter.

  4. Grow from there

    Track progress, add mentors as you go, and tell us what you need next.
