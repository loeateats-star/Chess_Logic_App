From first move to real calculation.

Kids start wherever they are. Puzzles get harder as their streak grows, and every level stays free.

  1. New to chessLearn how each piece moves with drag-and-drop lessons.
  2. BeginnerShort tactics: pins, forks and skewers, rated under 1000.
  3. IntermediateTwo- and three-move combinations, rated 1000 to 1600.
  4. AdvancedDeep calculation and endgames, rated above 1600.

Seven ways to practise.

All free, all in the browser

  • Chess basics Start here How every piece moves, taught through drag-and-drop exercises.
  • Puzzle trainer 6.1M puzzles Real positions from real games, served at your level as your streak grows.
  • Special rules Castling and more Check, checkmate, castling and en passant: the rules that trip up every beginner once.
  • Puzzle Rush 1 to 3 min Solve as many puzzles as you can before the clock runs out. Beat your best.
  • Video lessons 4 tiers Playlists from trusted chess teachers. The site remembers what you have watched.
  • Play live Via Lichess Connect a Lichess account and play a real opponent right on this board.
  • Game analysis Engine graded Paste a game and see your best moves, your mistakes and your blunders.
