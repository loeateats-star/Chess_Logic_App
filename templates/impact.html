Rogers Elementary.

Our first Title I school. We delivered five chess sets so a whole class could play at once, not take turns at one board.

  • Five sets, one classroom

    Enough boards for kids to play in pairs during a single lesson.

  • Free training at home

    Every student can keep practising with the puzzle trainer and video lessons, at no cost.

Christie Elementary.

Our second Title I school. Two more chess sets went to Christie, so more kids there can learn the game.

  • Two sets delivered

    Handed over in person to the school's staff.

  • Free training at home

    Students can keep practising with the puzzle trainer and video lessons, at no cost.

Out in the community.
